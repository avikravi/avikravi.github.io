Maker vs taker

Taker fills are orders that trade right away against an order already on the book. They pay Kalshi's trading fee.

Maker fills are limit orders that sit on the book until someone else trades against them.

On these bets, waiting for a price instead of taking one avoided the fee.

About

How the numbers are calculated

Cost includes the fees paid to open a bet. Payout is net of the fees paid to close it. Profit is payout minus cost.
