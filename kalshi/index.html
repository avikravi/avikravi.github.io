Maker vs taker

Taker fills are orders that trade right away against an order already on the book. They pay Kalshi's trading fee.

Maker fills are limit orders that sit on the book until someone else trades against them.

On these bets, waiting for a price instead of taking one avoided the fee.

About

How the numbers are calculated

Every bet reads like an equation. Contracts × price = stake; stake + buy fees = cost. Payout − sell fees = net payout. Net payout − cost = profit. Settled bets pay $1 per winning contract (or $0); bets sold early pay contracts × sale price. Open bets show what they'd fetch if sold now at the current bid, and their profit is unrealized.
