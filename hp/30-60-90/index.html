Role Vision Hypothesis

Manufacturers and robotics teams want vision AI on the floor without sending production data to the cloud. This role makes ZGX Nano and Fury the obvious way to do that.

Risks & Dependencies Hypothesis

  • Team structure
    Unclear lanes with adjacent PMM, technical marketing and partner teams would slow the first 30 days.
  • Resources & hardware access
    Credible vision AI positioning means running it. I need early Nano access, time on a Fury, and a small demo budget.
  • Stakeholder alignment
    Product, Sales, partners and NVIDIA each pull the story differently. The plan assumes one agreed owner for messaging calls.

Also Worth Exploring Question

  • Academic channel. Place ZGX Nano units with 1–2 labs, starting with Rice, for co-published benchmarks. Confirm: appetite for a formal program?
  • OEM reality. HP is one of 6+ OEMs (ASUS, Dell, Lenovo, MSI, Supermicro) on the same GB10 chip. Chassis, support and GTM execution differentiate, not the silicon.
  • NVIDIA dependency. NVIDIA sets the roadmap and, this year, DGX Spark pricing independent of OEMs. I'd set a standing quarterly NVIDIA touchpoint.
  • Positioning as a system. Formalize scrape → score → draft → review → publish, the loop already prototyped in the ZGX Nano case study. Confirm: does a version of this exist internally?
  • Re-baseline cadence. Revisit the roadmap every 4–6 weeks against fresh competitive signal. This market moved on price, a new entrant and a brand split in about twelve weeks this year alone.
