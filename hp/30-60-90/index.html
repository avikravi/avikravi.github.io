The 90-Day Arc

Days 1–30

  • Run a ZGX Nano end to end. Benchmark 3 open models (small, mid, large) via Ollama.
  • Verify the public review claims myself: memory bandwidth, idle power, context falloff.
  • Interview 8–10 stakeholders across Product, Engineering, Sales, and Channel.
  • Talk to 5+ real ZGX Nano users or developer-community members.
  • Stand up a living competitor tracker: every GB10 OEM plus real alternatives.
  • Take one Day 1 hypothesis to demo-ready.

Target100% of stakeholders interviewed, 3 models benchmarked, 1 prototype demo-ready.

Days 31–60

Build & Ship

  • Build the first ZGX Nano battlecard vs. every GB10 OEM and AMD's Ryzen AI Halo.

  • Extend hands-on coverage to Llama, Mistral, Gemma, and Qwen; evaluate vLLM.
  • Publish one benchmarked, fully-cited technical post.
  • Package the strongest Day 1 prototype into a customer-ready demo.
  • Run a GTM readiness review with Sales and channel partners.

  • NVIDIA dependency. NVIDIA sets the roadmap and, this year, DGX Spark pricing independent of OEMs. I'd set a standing quarterly NVIDIA touchpoint.
