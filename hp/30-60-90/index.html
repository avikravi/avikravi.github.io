Role Vision Hypothesis

Manufacturers want vision AI on the floor without sending production data to the cloud. ZGX Nano and Fury should be the obvious way to do that.

Year one
  • Clear position. On-prem vision AI for manufacturing and robotics.
  • Data sovereignty first. Images, models and results stay in the plant.
  • Crawl, walk, run. Prove one use case on a Nano, then scale to Fury.
  • Enabled field. Sales and partners carry the story with demos and battlecards.
