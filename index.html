About Me

Mechanical engineer turned computer vision researcher. A decade of product in between.

I started my career as a mechanical engineer at Oxy Oil & Gas. I built pipeline integrity risk models for assets in the Permian Basin and Colombia. That work made me curious about the software itself. I wanted to build tools, not just use them.

That curiosity led to almost a decade in product. Along the way, I learned how to ship, how to lead global teams, and how to build products people actually use.

Now I'm back at Rice, pursuing a Master's in Data Science, expected December 2026. I'm combining my engineering background, my product experience, and my technical depth in machine learning. This summer I completed a Computer Vision Research Internship with the Digital Health Institute at Rice University. I built AI pipelines that estimate tissue stiffness from video. This work supports autonomous surgical robots.
